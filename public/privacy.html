How It Works

Karmic Compass is operated by Diksha Dutt (operating as KarmicCompass). Here is what we collect, why, and how: • You give us: name, date of birth, gender, country, journal entries, chat messages, and optionally voice recordings and photos. • We generate: AI-powered insights, karma/dharma scores, astrological readings, quiz scores, and badges from your content. • Your voice audio is transcribed by Google's Gemini AI and immediately deleted from your device — only the text transcript is stored. • Photos you attach in Arya's chat are sent to Gemini Vision for AI processing and are not kept as files on our servers. Photos you attach to a journal entry ARE stored in our cloud storage for the life of your account, so we can show them back to you in your journal. • A stable anonymous device ID is stored on your device (persists across reinstalls). It enforces the one-trial-per-device policy, and a one-way hash of it travels with AI requests so a per-device anti-abuse cap can be applied — see §1(I). • Your passcode (if set) is stored only in your device's Keychain (iOS) / Keystore (Android) — never transmitted to us. • A background task runs every 12+ hours solely to reschedule local notifications — no data is sent to our servers during this. • We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising. Your content is not used to train any AI model. • You can delete your account and all data at any time from Settings.

3. Lawful Bases, Data Principles & Special Category Data

You may object to processing on this basis at any time. • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) / DPDPA / PDPA / APPI): where we rely on your consent — such as for notification permissions and the EU/UK 14-day withdrawal-waiver confirmation — you may withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing. How to withdraw, precisely: notification consent and crash-report consent are toggles in Settings. The Art. 9 health-data consent is different, and we would rather be plain about it than imply otherwise — journalling, mood and the conversations with Arya ARE the health-related processing, so it cannot be switched off while continuing to use those features. To withdraw the health-data consent entirely, delete your account (Settings → Delete Account), which erases the underlying data with it. • Explicit consent + Vital Interests (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a) primary; 9(2)(c) emergency fallback): see Crisis Flags below. • Legal Obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)): where processing is required by applicable law (e.g. financial record-keeping, regulatory demands). Special Category & Sensitive Data (GDPR Art. 9 / UK GDPR / equivalent): The App processes mental-health-adjacent content (journal entries, mood check-ins, emotional patterns, crisis signals) and health-adjacent inferences (karma/dharma scores, psychological themes). Under GDPR Art. 9 and equivalent laws, this qualifies as health-related sensitive personal data. We process such data on the basis of your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)). That consent is obtained through a dedicated consent screen shown during onboarding, before any journal, mood, or AI feature is used — it explains that your entries may contain health-related data and requires your affirmative agreement to proceed; it is a separate, specific act and is not bundled into general terms acceptance. Crisis-flag storage is also covered by this Art. 9(2)(a) explicit consent (with a dedicated "Safety follow-up memory" opt-out); we rely on Art. 9(2)(c) "vital interests" only as a narrow fallback in a genuine emergency where consent cannot be obtained (see below). You may withdraw your Art. 9(2)(a) consent at any time by deleting your account. Crisis flags (health data under GDPR Art. 9): The crisisFlags record (up to 100 characters of your message + timestamp) is written to your profile when your message matches a safety keyword. We process this primarily under your Art. 9(2)(a) explicit consent (with the "Safety follow-up memory" toggle as a granular opt-out), relying on Art. 9(2)(c) "vital interests" only where you are in a genuine emergency and cannot give consent, so we can surface immediate safety resources in future sessions. 4. AI Processing & Gemini API — Exact Data Sent

All AI requests are routed through our secure proxy server (Google Cloud Run, us-central1). The proxy applies a regex-based PII filter to scrub email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, and SSN patterns from text before forwarding to Google's AI models. Safety filters (BLOCK_MEDIUM_AND_ABOVE for harassment, hate speech, sexually explicit, and dangerous content) are applied to every request server-side. The proxy does not permanently store message content. Primary AI provider: Google Cloud Vertex AI (us-central1), accessed via Application Default Credentials. Fallback: Google Gemini Developer API (API-key based). Vertex AI customer content is excluded from Google's model training per Google Cloud Terms (https://cloud.google.com/terms/data-processing-addendum). Below is the exact data sent to Gemini for each feature: (a) ARYA CHAT (mentor_chat): The system prompt includes — your name, date of birth, gender, country, stated intention, karma/dharma scores, recent mood history, AI-generated life digest, inferred topic preferences, unresolved emotional threads, recent challenge data, starred message count, feedback context, active commitments (up to 3), personal notes you asked Arya to remember (up to 50), session history summaries, chat memory summary, last karma snapshot, recent journal entries (up to 30, capped at 600 chars each), AI-generated insights, and your selected reply language. Up to 40 recent chat messages are included as conversation history. Your current message (with a language instruction prefix) is the user turn. If you attach an image, it is included as inline base64 data. (b) VOICE TRANSCRIPTION (journal_transcribe): Your audio recording as inline base64 data, with a transcription instruction. No profile data is included. (c) JOURNAL ANALYSIS (journal_analysis): Your journal entry text, profile fields (name, dob, gender, country, intention), quiz history, earned badges, karma points, and recent journal entries. (d) DAILY INSIGHT / WEEKLY NARRATIVE / LIFE REPORT (daily_insight, report_generation): Journal excerpts, AI digest fields, chat memory, and session data. (e) ARYA MEMORY (memory_summary, memory_digest_update): Your psychological digest, recent journal excerpts, and existing memory summary. (f) HOROSCOPE (horoscope): Your zodiac sign (derived from date of birth) and the current date. No journal or chat content. (g) QUIZ GENERATION (quiz_generation, quiz_generation_hard): No personal data included — general thematic context only. (h) EXPORT — OPTIONAL ARYA LETTER: A journey summary (scores, digest overview) is sent to Gemini. Raw journal entries are not included. All API calls use Firebase ID token authentication (Bearer token in Authorization header). App Check tokens are attached where available. Requests use TLS encryption in transit.

5. Data Export

The "Export Data" feature in Settings generates a PDF report containing your journal entries, chat history with Arya, wellness summary, badges, and scores. If you choose to include an optional personalised cover letter from Arya, generating that letter requires an additional AI processing call to Google's Gemini API, using a summary of your journey (not raw journal text). The rest of the PDF is generated entirely on-device from your locally cached data without any network requests. The exported file is unencrypted. We do not transmit or receive the exported file on our servers. Once shared, you are solely responsible for the security and further distribution of that file. This Export Data feature is how we fulfil data-portability requests (GDPR Art. 20, LGPD, Quebec Law 25, Singapore PDPA): it provides your data in a portable PDF. The PDF is human-readable rather than a structured machine-readable file; if you need a machine-readable copy to transfer to another service, email privacy@karmiccompass.app and we will provide one.

11. Account Deletion

You can delete your account via two routes: (a) In-app: Settings → Account → Delete Account (requires active login). This calls our deleteAccount Cloud Function. (b) Email-based: if you cannot log in, contact app.karmiccompass@gmail.com or use the "Request Account Deletion" option in the App. We send a 6-digit verification code to your registered email (valid 15 minutes). For abuse prevention, your IP address is SHA-256 hashed (truncated to 32 hex characters) and stored briefly to rate-limit OTP requests; the raw IP is never persisted. Upon confirmation, our confirmDeletion Cloud Function processes the deletion. What happens when you confirm (both routes): your account is deactivated straight away — your sign-in sessions are revoked and your account is scheduled for erasure. Deletion is not instant by default: there is a 7-day grace period, and the data listed below is permanently erased from our active systems by a scheduled job once those 7 days have passed. The grace period exists so a deletion made by mistake, or by someone else with access to an unlocked phone, can still be undone. Cancelling during the 7 days: we email you a one-click cancellation link when the deletion starts. You can also sign back in during the grace period — the App will show you a screen offering "Restore my account". Deletion is the default: simply signing in does NOT cancel it, and if you do nothing (or choose "Continue with deletion") the erasure goes ahead on schedule. After the 7 days the deletion cannot be reversed. Deleting immediately: the in-app Delete Account flow also offers a "Delete now" option, which skips the grace period entirely and erases everything listed below straight away, including your Firebase Authentication account. Choose this if you need the data gone today — it cannot be undone. Data deleted (in both cases, once erasure runs): • All documents in the users/{uid} subcollections: entries, entriesArchive, chatArchive, insights, reports • aiUsage/{uid}, aiUsageJournal/{uid}, aiUsageMeter/{uid}, ttsUsage/{uid} and rateLimits/{uid} documents • deletionRequests/{uid} document • All documents in feedback where uid matches • All documents in chatReports where uid matches (AI-response reports you submitted) • deviceTrials and deviceTrialLedger: your account identity (UID) is scrubbed from the device records (the pseudonymous one-trial-per-device markers themselves are retained for abuse prevention — see §9) • The users/{uid} main document (entire profile, including push tokens and crisisFlags) • Journal image files stored in our cloud storage (Firebase Storage, users/{uid}/journalImages/) • Your Firebase Authentication account What is not deleted by name: three device- or IP-keyed anti-abuse records (aiUsageByDevice, rateLimitsByIp, processedWebhookEvents) cannot be looked up by account, because the server holds only a one-way hash rather than your identifier. They are not searched for and erased; they expire on their own timers instead — see §9 for each period. Residual data in encrypted Google Cloud backups may persist for up to 90 days. Subscription billing records are retained per RevenueCat and applicable financial law.
